Industry has three pillars

Two help organize your work life. One helps connect you to the wider service community.

Utility Schedule Your shifts, coverage needs, available hours, and workweek.

This is a usability test of an early Industry prototype for restaurant and service workers.

The workers, workplaces, shifts, jobs, events, and activity you'll see are simulated so you can experience how Industry could work.

We have one task for each pillar: Schedule, Jobs, and People.

Explore each task as naturally as you can. We want to learn what makes sense, what's confusing, and what could make Industry more useful for service workers.
