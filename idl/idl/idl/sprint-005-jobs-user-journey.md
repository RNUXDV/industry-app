# Sprint 005 — Jobs User Journey

**Date:** 2026-07-31

**Status:** In Progress

---

# Purpose

Define the emotional and functional journey a worker experiences inside the Jobs pillar before implementation begins.

Jobs is not a traditional job board.

Industry helps service-industry workers explore opportunities, prepare for change, confidently make a career move, and manage that transition.

---

# Design Philosophy

Jobs should be the most encouraging space inside Industry.

Finding work can be stressful, uncertain, and emotionally draining. Industry should respond by reducing anxiety rather than increasing it.

Every interaction should help workers feel seen, supported, and in control of their next step.

The goal is not simply to help someone find a job.

The goal is to help them feel hopeful about what's possible.

---

# Core Principle

Jobs is a career companion.

Not a classifieds page.

Every feature should help the worker move confidently toward a better opportunity.

---

# User Mindsets

Workers enter Jobs with different intentions.

Industry should recognize these mindsets rather than assuming everyone wants to leave their current job.

## Explore

"I'm curious what's available."

Goals

- Browse opportunities
- Compare workplaces
- Understand the market
- Save interesting jobs

---

## Prepare

"I may be ready for something new."

Goals

- Build a professional profile
- Update resume
- Create cover letters
- Define work preferences
- Organize experience

---

## Jump

"I'm ready."

Goals

- Apply confidently
- Submit applications
- Prepare for interviews
- Commit to a new opportunity

---

## Track

"I'm actively changing jobs."

Goals

- Track applications
- Monitor interviews
- Receive offers
- Manage next steps

---

# User Journey

↓

↓

Workers may enter at any stage.

Industry should support movement in both directions.

---

# Explore

Purpose

Discover opportunities without pressure.

Possible Features

- Opportunity Feed
- Nearby Jobs
- Event Gigs
- Saved Jobs
- Workplace Discovery

---

# Prepare

Purpose

Build confidence before applying.

Possible Features

- Worker Profile
- Resume Builder
- Cover Letter Builder
- Skills
- Certifications
- Availability
- Work Preferences

---

# Jump

Purpose

Transition from preparation to action.

Possible Features

- Readiness Check
- Apply
- Interview Preparation
- Employer Contact

---

# Track

Purpose

Manage the application journey.

Possible Features

- Applied
- Interview Scheduled
- Follow-up
- Offer Received
- Accepted

---

# Work Preferences

Industry should understand more than qualifications.

Potential preference categories include

- Restaurant Style
- Team Size
- Work Pace
- Shift Preference
- Neighborhoods
- Commute
- Leadership Style
- Workplace Culture
- Career Goals

These preferences will improve recommendations throughout Jobs.

---

# Design Principles

Jobs should feel hopeful.

Jobs should never pressure someone to leave their current employer.

Workers should feel supported whether they are browsing, preparing, or actively seeking new employment.

Every interaction should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

# Emotional Design Goal

Jobs should leave people feeling more hopeful than when they arrived.

Every interaction should reduce anxiety, increase confidence, and remind workers that they have options.

Industry does not pressure people into changing jobs.

It helps them make thoughtful career decisions at their own pace.
