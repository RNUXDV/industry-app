# Sprint 005 — Jobs Architecture

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Status:** In Progress

## Objective

Define the Jobs pillar’s purpose, information architecture, data model, user flows, states, and reusable components before implementation.

## Product Role

Jobs helps service-industry workers discover relevant opportunities, understand whether a workplace fits their needs, and move from discovery to application without unnecessary friction.

## Initial Questions

- What should the Jobs dashboard show first?
- How will workers search and filter opportunities?
- What information must every job card contain?
- What makes Industry’s job experience different from generic job boards?
- How will saved jobs and application status work?
- How should location and neighborhood discovery work?
- Which Schedule components can be reused?
